Fair questions.

On your list, we act as a data processor: it stays yours, and we only use it to do this job for you.

Is it legal?

Yes, when it's done properly. UK rules let you text people who asked you for a quote or bought from you, about similar work, as long as they could opt out then and can opt out now.

Every message carries an opt-out and opt-outs are honoured straight away, for good. We go through your list with you first, and leave out anyone we shouldn't text.

Will it annoy people?

A short, friendly text from a business they asked for a quote rarely does. We keep it to a handful of messages and stop the moment someone says no.

My list's a mess. Does that matter?

No. Send it in whatever state it's in. We tidy it, remove duplicates and anyone who's opted out, and check it with you before anything goes out.

How fast will I see bookings?

Replies start as soon as the first texts land. How many turn into bookings depends on your list, so we won't promise a number we can't back up.
