Fair questions.

The ones every owner asks on the call. Anything else, ask us straight.

Is texting my old customers legal?

Yes, when it's done properly. UK rules let you text people who enquired with you or bought from you about similar work, as long as they could opt out then and can opt out now.

Every message we send carries an opt-out, and opt-outs are honoured straight away, for good. We go through your list with you before anything goes out.

Will it sound like a robot?

No. It's written with you, in your words, and sent from your number. If you'd say "alright", it says "alright". You sign off the wording before anything goes out.

What if someone replies with something awkward?

Anything odd, a complaint, or a question it can't answer properly gets passed straight to you. It doesn't argue and it doesn't make up prices.

Do I need to change my CRM or systems?

No. We work with what you've got, even if that's a spreadsheet and a paper diary. If you want something proper later, we can build it.

What does it cost once the no-win-no-fee bit's done?

It depends what you keep running. We'll give you a fixed price on the call, before you commit to anything.

How quickly will I see booked appointments?

Replies start as soon as the first texts land. How many turn into bookings depends on your list, so we won't promise a number we can't back up.

What happens if nothing comes of it?

You don't pay. That's the deal with no-win-no-fee: we take the risk on the first run, not you.
