Privacy policy.

What we collect, why, who helps us process it, and how to make us stop. Last updated 4 November 2026.

Who we are

Surge Lab Ltd ("we", "us") runs surgelab.co.uk. We are the data controller for information you give us through this site, by text or by phone. Company number 16407386, registered in England and Wales at 34 Lulworth Place, Warrington, WA4 6FG.

Questions about your data: info@surgelab.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, business, mobile number, email and anything you write in the form.
  • Texts and calls: messages you send us or receive from us, call recordings and call summaries where calls are recorded. Callers are told when a call may be recorded.
  • Chat: if you use a chat assistant on this site, what you type into it.
  • Bookings: the details you give when booking a call.
  • Analytics: pages visited and buttons clicked, only if you accept analytics cookies.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a call (legitimate interests, or steps before a contract).
  • To text you about your enquiry, only if you ticked the box to say we can (consent).
  • To deliver services to our clients (contract).
  • To understand how the site is used, only with your consent.

We never sell your data and never use it to market unrelated products.

Texts and AI processing

Some of our text replies, and any chat assistant on this site, are written with the help of AI. An AI assistant always says so when a conversation starts, and a person can take over at any time. Messages are processed by our AI provider to draft replies and, under our provider's commercial terms, are not used to train its models.

Every text we send carries an opt-out. Reply STOP and you won't hear from us by text again.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and chat conversations that don't become clients: 12 months.
  • Client records: 6 years after the work ends, as required for business and tax records.
  • Call recordings, where we record calls: 90 days.
  • Opt-outs: kept permanently, so we never text you again.

Client lists

When we run reactivation or follow-up for a client, the client is the data controller for their customer list and we act as their data processor. We only use that list to do the job they hired us for. Every message carries an opt-out, and opt-outs are honoured instantly and permanently across all our systems.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent at any time. Email info@surgelab.co.uk and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
