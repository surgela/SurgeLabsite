Who we are

Surge Lab Ltd ("we", "us") runs surgelab.co.uk. We are the data controller for information you give us through this site, by text or by phone. Company number TBC, registered at TBC. ICO registration number TBC.

Questions about your data: info@surgelab.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, business, mobile number, email and anything you write in the form.
  • Texts and calls: messages you send us or receive from us, call recordings and call summaries where calls are recorded. Callers are told when a call may be recorded.
  • Chat: what you type into the chat assistant on this site.
  • Bookings: the details you give when booking a call.
  • Analytics: pages visited and buttons clicked, only if you accept analytics cookies.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a call (legitimate interests, or steps before a contract).
  • To text you about your enquiry, only if you ticked the box to say we can (consent).
  • To deliver services to our clients (contract).
  • To understand how the site is used, only with your consent.

We never sell your data and never use it to market unrelated products.

Texts and AI processing

Our chat assistant and our text replies are written with the help of AI. The chat makes this clear when it opens, and a person can take over at any time. Messages are processed by our AI provider to draft replies and are not used to train their models confirm against provider terms.

Every text we send carries an opt-out. Reply STOP and you won't hear from us by text again.
