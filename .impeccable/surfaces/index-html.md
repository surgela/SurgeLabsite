## Scope

Home page (index.html) and the Phase 1 pages that share its system: reactivation.html, book.html, privacy.html, cookies.html, terms.html. Mode: Persuade. Code-led (no image generation on this machine).

## Audience and action

UK trade and clinic owners on a phone between jobs. Action: book a 15-minute call. Proof: the site itself (instant text-back, chatbot), example threads labelled as examples, no-win-no-fee. No real case study yet: the slot is built and marked for the team.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The business that keeps working after you knock off. The page refuses the agency hero (headline left, product shot right, logo bar) and makes a phone lock screen at night the first thing you see, filling up with real work arriving.

OWN-WORLD: Van livery and tape measure. Deep navy ground like a sign-written van at night, cyan as the brand stripe and live signal, tape-measure yellow only on the booking button. Archivo Expanded heavy for headlines, set big like lettering on a van side; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for body, readable on a phone in daylight. Thick 2px rules like a job sheet, square corners on buttons, soft rounded only on phone notifications.

STORY: A visitor sees their phone doing the work at 11:42pm, Sunday, 6:15am. They read "More work in. More time back.", see the three pillars, see an old quote come back to life, learn there is no risk, and book.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy. "More work in." huge, top left. A phone, large, centred and cropped by the bottom edge, its lock screen clock moving through the week as five notifications stack. "More time back." huge, right, lower, crossing the phone's edge. Subline and the yellow "Book a 15-minute call" button beneath line two; secondary "See what's in your old leads".

FORM: Brief-pinned direction (lock screen hero, navy and cyan, trade feel); concept-seed not run because the brief pins it. Signature interaction: notification stack driven by GSAP with the lock screen clock advancing; reduced motion shows the finished stack. Motion only in the hero, calculator count-up, and one section reveal.

seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
